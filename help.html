Advanced Features

How Pro Features Actually Work

🌪️ Instant Server-Side Cloning

Normally, if someone sends you a public Google Drive link (like a massive 50GB game file or video course), you have to download it to your PC, and then manually re-upload it to your own Drive. This takes hours and ruins your data cap.

With the Pro Bot, you just paste that public link into Telegram. The bot instructs Google's servers to copy the file directly to your Drive. Because the transfer happens inside Google's internal data centers, it bypasses your home network completely. A 50GB file will clone into your Drive in roughly 1 to 2 seconds.

🎬 Secure Web Uploads & VLC Streaming

Telegram has strict limits on file sizes and download speeds. If a friend needs to send you a massive raw video file, don't make them use Telegram or WhatsApp. Click "Secure Web Upload" in the Pro bot.

It gives you a custom webpage link. Your friend opens it on their browser and uploads the file at full speed. It goes directly into your Google Drive.

The VLC Magic: Once uploaded, the bot generates a Direct Download link for you. If it's a video file, you don't even need to download it. Open the VLC Media Player app, go to "Open Network Stream," and paste our direct link. You can stream the massive 4K video instantly without taking up a single megabyte of local storage!

Security & Infrastructure

The Philosophy of BYOC

Why we ask you to Bring Your Own Credentials.

Why BYOC? If we hosted 10,000 users on a single, central Google Cloud Project, Google would severely throttle our API limits. Everyone's uploads would slow to a crawl, and centralizing that much access is a privacy nightmare. BYOC (Bring Your Own Credentials) turns you into your own server admin. You get dedicated, unthrottled API bandwidth, and absolute privacy because your data flows strictly between Telegram and your personal Google Cloud project. We never see it.

What exactly are Client IDs and Secrets? Think of a Client ID as a username or a digital badge that identifies your app to Google ("Hi, I am DS GDrive"). The Client Secret is the password that proves your app is legitimate. Together, they authorize the bot to ask you for Drive access.

⚠️ The Risks of Leaking

While having just the JSON file isn't enough to steal your files (an attacker still needs you to click the Google Login approval screen), you must treat the downloaded credentials JSON file like a bank PIN. If leaked alongside an active token, bad actors could hijack your API quotas or potentially access your Drive. Never share your credentials.json file with anyone, not even our support team.

The Ultimate Guide

Configuring Google Cloud

Follow this detailed, foolproof guide to perfectly set up your Google Cloud Console without getting stuck on Unverified App errors or expiring tokens.

Step 1: Create the Project & Enable APIs

  1. Open the Google Cloud Console and sign in.
  2. Click the project dropdown in the top header and click New Project. Name it DS GDrive Pro and hit Create. Wait a few seconds, then make sure it is selected in the top bar.
  3. Open the left navigation menu (hamburger icon) > APIs & Services > Library.
  4. Search for Google Drive API, select it, and click the blue Enable button.

Step 2: The OAuth Consent Screen

Go to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen. Choose External and click Create. Fill in the App Name (DS GDrive Pro) and add your email to the two required support/developer email boxes. Save and continue to the end.

Crucial: The "Testing" vs "Published" Dilemma

Many users get stuck here. You have to make a choice on the summary dashboard regarding your "Publishing status". Read carefully to choose what is best for you.

Option A: Leave it in "Testing"
  • ✅ Pros: You will not see any scary "Unsafe App" warnings when you log in. It is a smooth login process.
  • ❌ Cons: Google forces Testing tokens to expire every 7 days. You will have to click the login link inside Telegram once a week to keep the bot working.
If you choose Testing: You MUST scroll down to "Test Users", click "Add Users", and type in your exact Gmail address. If you don't do this, Google will reject your login.
Option B: Click "Publish App"
  • ✅ Pros: The login token lasts forever. You log in once, and the bot will work flawlessly for years without interruption.
  • ❌ Cons: Because you are publishing an app requesting Drive access without paying for a corporate audit, Google will throw a massive red "Unverified App" warning when you log in. (We explain how to safely bypass this below).
Highly Recommended: We strongly advise clicking "Publish App". Bypassing the warning is easy, and avoiding weekly logouts is worth it.

Step 3: Creating Credentials

  1. Go to Credentials in the left sidebar.
  2. Click + Create Credentials at the top and choose OAuth client ID.
  3. CRITICAL: In the "Application type" dropdown, you MUST select "Desktop app". If you select "Web application", the bot will break.
  4. Click Create. A window will pop up. Click Download JSON. Keep this file safe.
  5. Open @DS_GDrive_Pro_bot, run /set_creds, and upload this file.

Step 4: Bypassing the "Unverified App" Warning

If you chose to "Publish" your app in Step 2, clicking the login link in Telegram will take you to a scary page that says "Google hasn’t verified this app."

Why does this appear? Google flags any published app asking for Drive access to protect users from phishing scams.

Why doesn't the developer remove it? To remove this screen, a developer must pay thousands of dollars for an annual third-party security audit (CASA Tier 2). Since this BYOC project is entirely for your own personal use, paying for an audit is impossible and unnecessary.

Is it safe? Yes! It is 100% safe because you are the developer of the app. You are simply authorizing the Cloud Project you just created 5 minutes ago.

How to Bypass It:
  1. On the warning screen, look at the bottom left and click the small text that says "Advanced".
  2. More text will expand downwards. Click the link that says "Go to DS GDrive Pro (unsafe)".
  3. Click Continue on the final authorization screen. You are done!
