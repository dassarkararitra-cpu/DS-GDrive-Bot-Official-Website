Pro Setup: Bring Your Own Credentials (BYOC)

To give the Pro bot full access to your Drive securely, you must generate your own API credentials in Google Cloud.

Security Note: Are my Client ID and Secret safe?

Yes. The Client ID and Client Secret do not contain your personal files or passwords. They simply act as a "nametag" for your bot to talk to Google. Having these keys alone is not sufficient to access your account. After providing them to the bot, you will still undergo a secure Google Login screen where you explicitly grant permission (OAuth token). Download your JSON file and keep it safe; it's required to run your Pro engine.

1

Create a Google Cloud Project

Go to the Google Cloud Console. Click the project dropdown at the top and select New Project. Name it and click Create.

2

Enable the Google Drive API

Navigate to APIs & Services > Library. Search for "Google Drive API" and click Enable.

3

Configure OAuth Consent

Go to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen. Choose "External" and fill out the required app name and email fields. Save and continue.

4

Create Credentials (Desktop Application)

Navigate to Credentials. Click + Create Credentials > OAuth client ID.
Important: Under Application Type, you MUST select Desktop application. Name it and click Create.

5

Download JSON

A popup will appear with your Client ID and Secret. Click the Download JSON button. You will provide this file to the Pro bot to establish your secure connection.
