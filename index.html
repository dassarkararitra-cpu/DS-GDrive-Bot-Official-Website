Everything You Need in One Bot

DS GDrive Bot connects your Telegram account to Google Drive seamlessly. Here is what you can do straight from your chat.

1

Secure Google Login

Connect your account safely. The bot uses verified Google login, meaning you never share your password with us. Once connected, your session stays active so you don't have to keep logging in.

2

Direct File Uploads

Forward documents, photos, audio, or videos into the chat. The bot instantly uploads them directly into your secure Google Drive folder while showing you live progress.

3

Instant File Cloning

Send a public Google Drive link to the bot to clone the file instantly. It copies directly from Google's servers to your Drive, bypassing your phone's network completely.

4

Secure Temporary Links

Generate fast, secure download links for your files without exposing your actual Google Drive account. These links automatically expire after 24 hours for added privacy.

5

File Management

Browse your cloud files directly in Telegram. Create new folders, set default upload destinations, rename files, and toggle access between Public and Private with a tap.

6

Storage & Trash Management

Check your total storage limit and available free space instantly. The bot also includes a Trash Bin feature so you can easily restore accidentally deleted files or empty the trash permanently.
