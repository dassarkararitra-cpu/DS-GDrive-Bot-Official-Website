1. Service Architecture & Operating Models

The DS GDrive ecosystem operates across two distinct operational models designed for user privacy and data sovereignty:

A. Standard Bot (@DS_GDrive_bot) — Verified Minimal Scope:

The standard bot is an application utilizing our verified Google OAuth credentials. It is strictly constrained to the restricted https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. It can only access, modify, or view files that were directly uploaded or created through the bot itself. It is technologically impossible for the standard bot to inspect, view, or alter preexisting personal files on your Google Drive.

B. Pro Bot (@DS_GDrive_Pro_bot) — Bring Your Own Credentials (BYOC):

The Pro bot delivers comprehensive Drive management (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) exclusively through a Bring Your Own Credentials (BYOC) infrastructure. You independently create and administer a personal Google Cloud Console project, generating your own unique Client ID and Client Secret. You are the legal and technical developer of your own Google Cloud application. DS GDrive Pro operates purely as a client-side automation engine executing routines at your direct instruction using your own self-managed credentials.
